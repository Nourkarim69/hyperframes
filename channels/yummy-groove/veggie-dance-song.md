# The Veggie Dance Song (Yummy Groove)

Original song, call-and-repeat structure (no melody or lyrics borrowed from Baby Shark).
Style: upbeat kids pop, cheerful children's voices, 120 BPM, C major, marimba + claps + bouncy bass.
Each verse is one action the child copies (jump, roll, pop, wiggle, spin).

[Chorus]
Veggie, veggie, dance dance dance!
Wiggle-wiggle, wiggle-wiggle, dance dance dance!
Veggie, veggie, dance dance dance!
Everybody dance like a veggie!

[Verse 1]
Carrot jumps, crunch crunch crunch!
Carrot jumps, crunch crunch crunch!
Carrot jumps, crunch crunch crunch!
Jump like Carrot!

[Chorus]

[Verse 2]

Tomato rolls, round and round!

[Verse 3]

[Chorus]

[Verse 4]

[Verse 5]

[Chorus]

[Outro]

Veggies, veggies, yum yum yum!
Bye bye, veggies!
