# Pip & Friends - series bible

**Tagline:** Bedtime adventures in Whispering Woods.
**Format:** English, 3–5 min episodes (16:9) + 5–6 Shorts each (9:16). Ages 2–6. Calm, warm, funny, always ends cosy.

## Places

Pip's burrow (round door, glowing window), the forest path, Ollie's big tree, the tallest hill, the pond.

## Episode ideas

1. **Pip and the Fallen Star** - sharing joy lifts a friend up. _(done)_
2. Pip and the Storm Knock - a lost baby rabbit; being brave for someone smaller.
3. Hazel's Prickly Problem - Hazel thinks her spikes keep friends away; everyone is different.
4. The Moon Who Couldn't Sleep - helping someone feel calm; bedtime routine.
5. The Great Acorn Race - winning isn't everything; cheering for friends.
6. Ollie Loses His Glasses - asking for help is OK.
7. The Firefly Lantern - sharing your light.
8. Pip's First Snow - trying new things.
9. The Secret of the Pond - patience.
10. Twinkle's Birthday - giving gifts from the heart.
